I wrote a story a while back, but I thought it might be interesting for others to read.

In days past people did not die. When they were apart from one another each forgot the other so that when they met again it was as if things occurred for the first time. In this way the world was kept broad and new. As each person would move from place to place, each acquaintance was new even if they had crossed each other’s paths many times over the years. There were good people and bad people, but because each only was providential to themselves the world was generally a happy place.

Coyote saw this and being the mischievous god that he was, he was inclined to change the nature of the world for fun and general irritation. So Coyote caused all the peoples of the world to fall asleep and when they awoke they had the ability to remember each other. When someone met another and they argued, or they were lovers or they were friends, each person remembered the time together.

Before Coyote, the world was a watercolor. Each morning and on every person’s heart the world was new. Washed clean and repainted every day. After the people woke up they had scars. Every joy, hurt, love and pain was recorded there on each person’s heart. It became an exercise, a trial each morning to wake up and face what was to come. Life was now effort.

But as with all gifts that Coyote has given to the people it was more than just mischief. Each person now had the ability to do lasting good, reverberating through other’s lives and growing the grass of life. And on the other side each person has the ability to pain another’s soul. To damage and scar another’s heart. Coyote gave us each the ability to do both, and the burden that it will be remembered. Choice and consequence are now part of every day that the world wakes up and begins anew.

Monocular depth estimation

This repo and associated paper shows how to use diffusion based methods for fine tuning and zero-shot learning to everyday photos. I think this is interesting as a upscale process for 3D photogrammetry methods. There is currently some inexpensive yet pretty capable 3D scanners on the market that I would love to play with, but using this method combines with cell phone pics would allow me to bring my racecar into 3D space at high resolution for something like Airshaper runs.
