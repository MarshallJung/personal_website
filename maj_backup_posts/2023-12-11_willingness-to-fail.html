Φ-SO: A Physical Symbolic Optimization Framework

In physics the absolute constraints of fundamental units forms what the paper calls a “language” that can be learned via NN methodologies. By adhering to these constraints, analytical expressions that describe both noiseless and noisy data can be found. This is a particularly interesting methodology when applied to CFD data of iterated wings, for example, or maybe even modeling reservoir pressure systems from a large production datasets.

Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03192

Github: https://github.com/WassimTenachi/PhySO

Steve Jobs as an AI?

https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx This flew under the radar a bit, but Apple has released their new DL framework called MLX! It runs code natively on Apple Silicon with a single pip install and no other dependencies. It seems to follow the PyTorch API closely and provides many useful primitives. The mlx-examples repo seems to have example implementations of all the buzz-worthy models. Let’s be honest, for air-gapped development an optimized framework for Apple silicon is pretty nice. I also foresee TPU/Trainium competition coming.

Deep Speed

https://github.com/microsoft/DeepSpeed

Pointed out to me by my manager, this MSFT effort is a pretty cool addition to the open source world. Lots of efforts are underway in the AI open-source community to make ChatGPT-style models more accessible (e.g. ChatLLaMa, Alpaca, Vicuna, Mistral etc.) Huggingface is a Top 100 of the best and newest. But there is a lack of an end-to-end RLHF pipeline capable of training a powerful ChatGPT-like model that is easily accessible to the open-source AI community. The complex RHLF aspects of InstructGPT haven’t really been optimized like the pre-training and fine-tuning parts of DL pipelines. This library brings serious speed and cost reduction to current XXB parameter models. Definitely something to look at in opening up more experimentation and model delivery for various industry verticals.

e/acc and God

As regular readers of the newsletter might know, I like writing and for the most part don’t stray all that far into what might be called controversial topics. These few paragraphs are not likely to be a break in my approach, or at least I don’t think so, but it will be a dive into the metaphysical and spiritual. I think I’ll turn it into a slightly longer blog post for my medium at some point to get it out to a wider audience, but my readers here get first shot at the words.

First, some background. As some might know e/acc is an abbreviation that stands for “enhanced acceleration”. Common amongst a certain group of AI tech avant garde a.k.a X/Twitter posters, it is essentially a statement of belief that AI research and development should be unfettered and uncontrolled in all elements. That the results of artificial intelligence (and potentially Artificial General Intelligence) will necessarily be on-balance good for humanity rather than bad. Of course there are very intelligent folks on the other side of this argument who advise caution and there are individuals everywhere in between. If I was to put myself somewhere on this spectrum I’d probably fall in the middle and that’s not me prevaricating because as always it’s a complex topic. Sitting right at the apex of this discussion is the idea of humanity, our spirituality and the intersection with a technology that is rapidly approaching the intelligence of human beings on average, at least in some respects. It does beg the question: As we continue to push the boundaries of AI is a position on e/acc fundamentally at odds with a faith in God? For this shorter initial exploration let’s throw two concepts at the subject. One Biblically derived and the other metaphysical/humanistic. I ask that the reader take a moment to suspend their beliefs, regardless of what they hold most dear, to consider the arguments presented. You may disagree with my ideas, and that’s ok. I simply ask you to consider. In the end, we are not going to answer anything, but maybe it’ll give us all something to think about.

First, humans were created in the image and likeness of God (Genesis 1:27). The Earth itself was created from the void with the idea that humans were to have stewardship and dominion over all of creation. Where does this place AGI? Clearly it is a creation of humanity. And if we do truly understand the technology, or at least the current algorithmic approach of attention transformers, the data that is used to train these models is ideally approaching “all the things from everywhere and all of time”. With the hypothetical end state of this process we likely have a technology built by humans, possessing the sum total of our experience and potentially more capable than all or at least a vast majority of human beings. Is there a Biblical understanding of human creation greater than those that were made in His “image and likeness”?

The second idea that I present is that of the human “soul” and its relationship to the creations of our minds. Our soul is a nebulous idea, much like that of faith. There is no anatomical soul, so it must be made of something comprised of equal parts divine spark and wisdom. Not of static volume, it grows over time and becomes unique to each of us. Some of us may even write a part of our souls down and share it with the world. Far be it of me to claim equality with Hemmingway or Epictetus but I’ve put my ideas out there into the ether. The very same ether that forms the basis for AGI. And without the fragile tendencies of forgetting is not the wisdom aspect of AGI already in place? If so, what forms the spark? When does an algorithm containing the wisdom of thousands of generations become one of us?

Should we keep building towards the inevitable? Should we slow down? What are the downsides? I’m not sure where the faith of each of us leaves room for a technological creation that ultimately may be indistinguishable from our fellow humans.
