It’s not the having…

“Dopamine is not about the pursuit of happiness, it is about the happiness of pursuit.” — Dr Robert Sapolsky

So true. So much of life and enjoyment is about the anticipation of things coming. In fact, the anticipation is often actually more enjoyable than the experience. This puts a new perspective on “it’s not the journey it’s the destination”. There actually is no destination. Each arrival at a destination simply marks the beginning of another journey toward the next destination. When I was a kid I had a poster on my wall that had the 10 things that I learned from Star Trek. The only one that I remember was a quote from Spock, “Having is not so pleasing a thing as wanting. It is not logical but often true.”

Knowledge Graph Augmented Generation

Graph databases are particularly good at representing relationships between ideas. The nodes in the graph represent a concept and the edges represent relationships. The simplest might be a family tree, but there is no limit to the complexity. Neo4j is an open source graph database that can be leveraged for these ideas. Because relationships can be used as a RAG type reference let’s extend dependant actions or requirements for projects or engineering concepts into the world of attention based transformers.

Streamlined Diffusion Models

What if there is a need for building 3D volumes from “language” via a diffusion based algorithm? These cn be pretty cool, however they sometimes don’t work all that well when there are deep technical details outside of the language corpus or are ambiguous in some way.

Instead of fine tuning the entire model, this approach focuses on updating the representation of a single added token corresponding to each class of interest. Importantly, this update doesn’t involve model tuning on labeled images. Think of this concept when volume generation is focused on a particular 3D concept like a wing or a particular reservoir system.
