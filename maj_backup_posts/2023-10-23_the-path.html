The Language of Embeddings

As I’ve mulled the idea of attention transformers the concept of what defines a language keeps emerging. While the industry has asymptotically approached the limits of current model architecture, barring a mathematical or silicon based breakthrough, the concept of carefully curating and embedding all language, no matter how esoterically defined, is where I see the future of the technology heading.
