Play the hand you’re dealt…

“If you had disadvantages — I agree with you. You are right. It’s harder to be successful if “X” happened to you. Replace “X” with: Gender, race, birth deformity, different language, different country, abuse, etc. The main point of the longer conversation is that despite the disadvantage you only have one choice: What are you gonna do about it?

  1. Take action anyway and become proof to other people — like you — your people — also born into or abused into this tragedy that you were that they too can overcome it.
  2. Blame and complain.

And to be clear — do whatever you want. I support your choice. But only one of those decisions will make you better. But you know who wins by you not being successful? Whoever or whatever you blame. But you can lead a rebellion of one. And blame the one thing you can control — which is YOU. In your mind, redefine the word “blame” as “give power to”. And when you do that, there’s only one person you’re going to want to give more power to — and that’s you. For everyone who had terrible circumstances — I’m on your side. Your long term side. The side that wants you to win. So do it anyway. With all the disadvantages. And still tell them to shove it. And win. I want be clear. If you had tough crap happen to you. It sucks. And it’s not your fault. But now what? Where do we go? My 2 cents — win anyways and prove that you can win even when the chips are stacked against you and you’re dealt a lousy hand. Because we can’t get dealt a new hand. We gotta play the cards we got rather than hoping the dealer rules in our favour. So again — what do you do with your hand? The only thing possible — you play it the best you can.” — Alex Hormozi

MSFT/OpenAI — CTRL, ALTman, Delete

I will not speculate…I will NOT speculate. OK, I will a little bit. What’s going on with Altman and OpenAI? In these cases I usually default to the balanced application of Occam and Hanlon’s. Is it because someone opened the door to the OpenAI basement and discovered this? Probably not, though sometimes the funniest explanation is the best. In reality though we can look at the market. $$ speak louder than anything. Google took a 10% hit when Q3 earnings warned on AI profitability. With OpenAI losing $$MM per day, sometimes a change of leadership is needed for profitability if it’s even achievable, which is far from certain. They may bring Altman back if it means the company brain trust remains, but have no doubt that as a for profit company (despite what’s written on the door) OpenAI will need to make $$ or smaller more focused disruptors will pick it to pieces.

Why you should use JAX

JAX is well suited for ML processes. In ML operations we often apply the same basic mathematical function across very large datasets. JAX handles this vectorisation very well in addition to having solid native support for TPUs which is a cheaper, scalable alternative to GPU’s.

What if someone drew it by hand?

Recently I came across a problem of recognising scanned or handwritten characters or drawings. One might simply say there are plenty of open source OCR tools available, but what if it’s digitizing a “back of the napkin” concept or digesting a set of curves or 3D images that don’t have a standard format? In this case GANs again can come to the rescue. ScrabbleGAN is a semi-supervised approach to learning handwritten images. Need to learn those pdf’s or .las’s? Give this a try.

https://github.com/Nikolai10/scrabble-gan
