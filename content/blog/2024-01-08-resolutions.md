### Moving from fine to extra-large fine?

So one of my complaints about the big multi-modal models is the lack of unique vertical understanding. It didn’t focus well on particular industry applications and more so on particular applications where there isn’t much public data. Well in this paper ([https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12832](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12832)) a new approach to late interactions is bit of blend between cross encoders and single vector with advantages from both. So is this now going to push fine tuned back towards larger models in general?
